Small Steps, Greener Planet

Learn how the 3 R’s and renewable energy can help us care for Earth. Every small action makes a difference!

The 3 R’s Renewable Energy Take Action

♻️ The 3 R’s

The 3 R’s help us reduce waste and use resources wisely.

🛍️

Reduce

Use less. Avoid unnecessary packaging, switch off unused lights and save water.

🧴

Reuse

Use items again instead of throwing them away. Carry a reusable bottle or bag.

♻️

Recycle

Sort recyclable paper, glass, metal and suitable plastics so they can become new products.

☀️ Renewable Energy

Renewable energy comes from natural sources that are replenished, such as sunlight, wind and moving water. It can help reduce pollution from fossil fuels.

☀️

Solar Energy

Solar panels turn sunlight into electricity.

🌬️

Wind Energy

Wind turbines use moving air to generate electricity.

💧

Hydropower

Moving water can spin turbines to produce electricity.

🌿 Small Ways to Help

🚰 Save water by turning off the tap when it is not needed.
💡 Switch off lights and electronics when you leave a room.
🚲 Walk, cycle or use public transport when practical.
🌳 Plant and care for trees and local plants.
Remember: You do not have to do everything at once. Choose one helpful habit and keep doing it!
